Jio IPO Terminal

Independent project, Semester 3, 2026

A research terminal built from Reliance Jio’s draft red herring prospectus. The filing is reorganised into the views an analyst actually reaches for: financial statements and disclosures, SEBI ratios and KPIs, a forecast simulator, spectrum holdings, shareholding, board and key managerial personnel, risk factors and litigation. Figures switch between rupees and dollars and filter by FY24, FY25 and FY26.

Underneath sits a 37-worksheet Excel data model, structured to load straight into Power BI. I developed it around a Finance Club workshop at IIM Sirmaur.

III

Research and coursework

Titan Company: regression analysis

Probability & Statistics, Semester 2, 2026

Sixty-plus quarters of Titan’s financials, FY2011 to FY2025, analysed with linear regression across six key performance indicators and validated with Excel’s Data Analysis ToolPak. I led the project as the course’s subject representative.

Netra: an AI early warning system for online narratives

Team project, problem statement SIH26152 (NTRO), 2026

A free, student-built social media analytics framework in Python, made by a team of eight to nine. It collects public posts, reads mood, audience and trending topics, maps who influences whom, and raises an alert when a narrative spikes or looks coordinated, with a Streamlit dashboard on top. I set up the team’s GitHub collaboration workflow.

Equity research: Kaynes Technology

Finserve Recruitment 2026, group of four

A group equity research presentation on Kaynes Technology within precision engineering. I presented the order book and execution, and the valuation.

Product life cycle analysis: IIM Sirmaur and IIM Ahmedabad

Operations Management, Semester 3, 2026, group of nine

A product life cycle study of IIM Sirmaur with IIM Ahmedabad as the primary benchmark. I owned the placements and alumni evidence.

Illegal sand mining on the Yamuna

Environment & Development, group presentation

A group study of illegal sand mining on the Yamuna near IIM Sirmaur. I covered what happens on the ground and the actors involved.

Growth accounting across four economies

Macroeconomics, Semester 2, 2026

A comparison of India, the USA, China and Israel using the Solow model with a Cobb–Douglas production function, applying growth accounting and regression in Excel to separate capital, labour and productivity contributions.
